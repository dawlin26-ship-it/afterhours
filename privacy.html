Privacy Policy

After Hours Studio · effective 30 September 2026

After Hours Studio is a private publishing tool used by the After : Hours music channel to post its own short jazz clips to its own TikTok, Instagram and X accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Information we receive

When the channel owner connects an account, the platform (for example TikTok) provides an access token and basic account details such as the account ID, display name and profile picture. These are used only to publish the channel's own videos to that same account and to confirm the account is connected.

How it is stored and shared

Access tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are never sold, rented or shared with anyone, and are sent only to the platform that issued them, when publishing.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking, no analytics about other people, and no access to any other user's data or content.

Keeping and deleting information

Access can be revoked at any time from the platform's own settings (on TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services). Once revoked, the stored token no longer works and is deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact After : Hours through the channel at https://www.youtube.com/@OriginalAfterHours.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
